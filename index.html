How each piece
comes to life


From material to your home-a journey of
intention at every step.

STEP 01


Source


We begin with materials-FSC-certified hardwoods from
managed Scandinavian forests.linens woven in Flanders.
and leathers tanned with chestnut bark.

STEP 02


Design


Every piece starts as a 1:1 clay or foam maquette .We test
proportions physically before a single linr is drawn
digitally.

STEP 03


Craft


Our Copenhagen atelier employs traditional joinery-no
screws in the frame.no staples in the upholstery.Habd-
tiedeight-way springs for the seat.

STEP 04


Deliver


White-glove delivery with in-home placement.ach piece
Arrives in reusable linen wrapping not cardboard.we take
the packaging back.
